Our next chapter · August 2026

Project Blue Star has joined The Melody Project!

As of August 2026, Project Blue Star has merged with The Melody Project, bringing our work into a broader national organization.

Our programs will continue operating as they do today. Organizers and students should continue using their existing contacts, workflows, and schedules as they currently.

Project Blue Star's Managing Director, Fyzeen Ahmad, and Outreach Director, Andrew Toensing, will continue supporting this work as members of The Melody Project's Advisory Board.

We are grateful to have served the Minnesota community since 2021, and for the students, families, volunteers, donors, and community partners who have made this work possible. We look forward to continuing to serve our community in this next chapter.
